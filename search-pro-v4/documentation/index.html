In Short

The whole path, in six steps. The Installation Guide has the details of each.

  1. Put the search-pro-v4 folder at the same level as your tour filesIt goes in the same folder as the rest of your tour, the folder that holds index.html. See Put the Folder in Your Tour.
  2. Add the search button in 3DVistaA button with one JavaScript action. See Add the Search Button.
  3. Add two lines to index.htmlThey load the search and your settings. See Add Two Lines to index.html.
  4. Upload the tour and the folderSee Upload to Your Server.
  5. Open the Control Panel and choose your settingsSee Open the Control Panel.
  6. Download your settings file search-pro-config.js and upload itVisitors see your settings after this step. See Save and Publish Your Settings.

What the Control Panel Has

One tab for each part of the search. The Settings Reference follows the same order.

Tab Use it to
General Choose how the search opens and closes, how it finds results, and where it sits on computers and phones
Appearance Choose the colors, corners and text of the search field and the results
Display Control how results look and what they show
Content Choose which elements of the tour visitors can find in the search
Filtering Choose which results are left out of the search, by name, tag or number
Data Sources Use a Google Sheet or a CSV file to change how items look in the search results; the tour itself is never changed
Animations Choose how the search bar, the results and the hover effects move
Advanced See what the search finds in your tour and tune how it matches what visitors type
Knowledge How to add the search to a tour, how your settings reach visitors, what each tab does and what to check when something does not work
